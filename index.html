About Jack

Hello, I’m Jack.

I’ve spent 12 years studying classical piano, now I love helping other people find the same enjoyment at the keys.

My teaching style is relaxed and goal-focused, with a real emphasis on how to practice and make steady progress. Every lesson is tailored to what you want to achieve, whether that’s playing your favourite songs, building confidence, or working towards an exam.

Lessons take place at the upright piano at my home in Withington, or I can travel to you and give lessons at your home.
