A few useful details

Frequently asked questions

Do you teach in Didsbury?

Yes. I teach across Didsbury, offering regular home visits as well as studio lessons just up the road in Withington. Lessons are relaxed and tailored to each student, with a focus on building confidence, enjoying learning and making steady progress. Home visits start from £30 per hour, or £27 at my Withington studio.

I have an old upright piano at home. Will it work for lessons?

Yes, in most cases. Lessons are shaped around your current level and what you would like to achieve. If the piano is badly out of tune or has keys that stick, let me know beforehand and we can talk it through.

What if I only have a keyboard?

That’s absolutely fine. The focus of lessons is on helping you develop your musical skills at a pace that suits you. If you ever want advice about equipment as you progress, I’m happy to help, with no pressure to upgrade.

Can my child have a lesson after school?

Yes, where availability allows. Send me the days and times that work for you and I’ll let you know what I have available.

Can I come to you instead?

Of course. Lessons at my home in Withington are £27 per hour, on an acoustic upright piano.
